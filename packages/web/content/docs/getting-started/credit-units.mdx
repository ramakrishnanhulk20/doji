---
title: Credit units and payouts
description: Claim your credit units, send them, sell or buy them before the date, take early payouts after it, and close and redeem a date.
---

When a borrower takes your offer, you are owed MUSD on that Thursday. Doji records the claim as credit units: 1 unit is 1 MUSD due on that date. Units are ERC-1155 tokens whose token id is the Thursday, so all units for one date on the same contracts are interchangeable. On [Lend](/lend), "Your credit units" has one card per date and per set of contracts: units from the 20 October contracts and from the Wave 2 contracts of 4 November 2026 are different tokens, even for the same Thursday.

## A date's card

The card's status follows the date:

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Before the Thursday. Loans at this date are still running. |
| Due, N loans still open | The Thursday has passed and the date is not closed yet. N is how many of its loans have not ended. |
| Closed | Every loan has ended and the date is final. |

| Row | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Units you hold | Units in your wallet for this date. |
| Waiting to be claimed | Units from your filled offers that are recorded but not minted yet. |
| In your withdrawal | Units you moved into an early payout, and what has been paid against them so far. Shown only once you have one. |
| Collected for this date | MUSD and BTC that repayments, auctions and settlements have paid in, against the MUSD due in total. |
| Value of one unit now | Collected MUSD and BTC divided by every unit at this date. |

## Claim units

When a borrower takes your offer, your units are written down in the contract but not minted, so nothing you control runs in the middle of someone's borrow. "Claim units" mints them to your wallet: "10.00 units for Thu 29 Oct are in your wallet."

## Send units

Before the date, "Send units" opens a small form: "Send to" takes an address (a wrong checksum or the zero address is refused) and "Units" takes an amount, with "Max". The button reads "Send 10.00 units". This is a plain ERC-1155 transfer: whoever holds the units is paid. To sell them for MUSD instead, list them.

## Sell units

On a Wave 2 date's card, "Sell units" appears while you hold units for it and more than a day is left before the Thursday. It opens "Sell units of" the date:

- "Units to sell", up to what you hold, with "Max".
- "Price per unit" in MUSD, above 0 and at most 1.000, because a unit never pays more than 1 MUSD.
- "Open until": "1 day", "3 days", "1 week", or until the Wednesday before the Thursday, which is the default.

A line under the price shows what the buyer earns and what you receive, for example "At 0.990 a buyer earns 1.01% by Thu 19 Nov, about ...% a year. You receive 0.9875 MUSD a unit after Doji's 0.25% fee." The one step, "List units", sends the units to UnitMarket with your price and expiry, and the app says, for example, "Listed 5.00 units of Thu 19 Nov at 0.990 MUSD each as listing #1."

Your listing then shows under "Credit units for sale" with "Your listing" and "Cancel listing", which brings the units left back at any time. Buyers pay you in the same transaction they buy in. After the expiry nobody can buy, and anyone can press "Return to seller" to send the units left back to you. Units in a listing are not in your wallet, so they cannot be sent, withdrawn early or redeemed until they come back.

A date from the 20 October contracts says "Units from the 20 October contracts can be sent, not listed." The rules are in [Selling credit units](/docs/concepts/selling-units).

## Buy units

"Credit units for sale", above "Your offers" on Lend, lists every open listing, soonest Thursday first and cheapest first, without a wallet. "Buy units" opens "Buy units of" the date: type the "Units to buy" and read "You pay", "You receive" and "You earn". The steps are "Approve" for exactly the cost, skipped if an earlier approval covers it, and "Buy units". When it lands: "Bought 2.00 units of Thu 19 Nov for 1.9800 MUSD. They are in Your credit units." They then work like any units you hold.

<Callout type="info">
The guardian can pause the units market. The list and both sheets then say "The units market is paused by Doji's guardian. Buying and selling resume when it ends. Cancelling a listing still works." Returning an expired listing works too.
</Callout>

## Early payouts, after the date

You do not have to wait for the last loan at a date to end. Once the Thursday has passed:

- "Withdraw what's collected" opens a sheet. Choose the "Units to move" (all of them by default) and it shows "You get now:" your share of the MUSD and BTC collected so far. "Withdraw" pays it.
- Moved units go into your withdrawal for that date. They can no longer be sold or sent, and you keep your share of what is still to come.
- As more is collected, "Withdraw the new part" pays what arrived since your last withdrawal. The card shows "Waiting for you:" with the amounts.

However and whenever you withdraw, each unit receives the same total within a few wei of rounding, so nobody gains by withdrawing first. How the share is worked out is in [Early payouts](/docs/concepts/early-payouts).

## Close and redeem

When the Thursday has passed and every loan at it has ended, the card shows "Close this date" with the note "Anyone can close a date once its last loan has ended." Closing freezes the totals that redemptions pay from.

Once closed, "Redeem 10.00 units" burns your units and pays your share, shown first as "You get ... MUSD and ... BTC". The MUSD part is at most 1 MUSD per unit. A unit is worth less than 1 MUSD only if a loan at that date fell short: a sale or settlement below the debt, or a position that left custody and was written off. If you had an early payout, "Withdraw the rest" pays what it is still owed.

<Callout type="info">
Part of a payout can be BTC. When nobody buys an auctioned position, the lenders at that date receive the Bitcoin itself at the oracle price, and BTC carries its own price risk until you sell it.
</Callout>
