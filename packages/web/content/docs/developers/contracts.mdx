## LoanManager

Custody of veBTC positions, loans, auctions and settlement. Every state-changing function runs under one reentrancy lock.

### Borrower functions

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deposit(uint256 tokenId)` | the position's owner | Pulls a veBTC position after a single-token approval and records the caller as its borrower. |
| `lockAndDeposit(uint256 amount, uint256 lockDuration)` | anyone | Locks the caller's BTC as a new veBTC position minted straight to LoanManager. Pausable. |
| `lockAndBorrow(uint256 amount, uint256 lockDuration, uint64 maturity, uint256[] offerIds, uint128[] amounts, uint16 maxAprBps)` | anyone | Locks and borrows in one transaction, with every borrow check. Pausable. |
| `borrow(uint256 tokenId, uint64 maturity, uint256[] offerIds, uint128[] amounts, uint16 maxAprBps)` | the borrower | Borrows MUSD from the listed offers against a held position with no open loan. Pausable. |
| `repay(uint256 tokenId, uint256 amount)` | anyone | Pays down a loan, at most what is owed. The last payment closes the loan and any auction. Never paused. |
| `roll(uint256 tokenId, uint64 newMaturity, uint256[] offerIds, uint128[] amounts, uint16 maxAprBps, uint256 maxTopUp)` | the borrower | Moves an active, on-time loan to a later Thursday and repays the old date in full. Never paused, but pays nothing out while borrows are paused. |
| `withdrawCollateral(uint256 tokenId)` | the borrower | Sends a position with no open loan back to its borrower. |
| `vote(uint256 tokenId, address[] pools, uint256[] weights)` | the borrower | Casts the position's PoolsVoter vote. The loan must be on time and safe, and a normal lock cannot vote in its last week. |
| `resetVotes(uint256 tokenId)` | the borrower | Clears the position's PoolsVoter vote. |
| `claimRewards(uint256 tokenId, address[] gauges, address[][] feeTokens, address[][] bribeTokens)` | the borrower | Claims pool fees and incentives from registered gauges and pays the measured increase of each whitelisted token to the borrower. |
| `claimRebase(uint256 tokenId)` | the borrower | Claims from the veBTC fee distributor. BTC from an expired lock is credited to the borrower's claimable. |
| `extendLock(uint256 tokenId, uint256 lockDuration)` | the borrower | Pushes the lock's end out with veBTC's `increaseUnlockTime`. The loan must be on time and safe. |

None of the pass-throughs (`vote` to `extendLock`) runs while the loan is in auction.

### Functions anyone can call

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `startAuction(uint256 tokenId)` | anyone | Starts the auction of an overdue or unsafe loan and snapshots its start value, floor, duration and penalty. |
| `cancelAuction(uint256 tokenId)` | anyone | Returns an LTV auction's loan to Active once a fresh price shows it safe. |
| `purchase(uint256 tokenId, uint256 maxPay, uint256 deadline)` | anyone | Buys an auctioned position at the current price and splits the payment between the market, the treasury and the borrower. |
| `prepareSettle(uint256 tokenId)` | anyone | Resets live votes and turns a permanent lock into a normal one on an unsold position past its floor time. |
| `settle(uint256 tokenId)` | anyone | Withdraws an unsold position's ended lock and splits the BTC between the market, the treasury and the borrower. |
| `writeOff(uint256 tokenId)` | anyone | Closes a loan whose position has left LoanManager's custody, with zero recovery. |
| `claim()` | anyone with a claimable | Pays the caller every MUSD and BTC claimable recorded for it. |

### Owner and guardian functions

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `setRiskParams(uint16 newMaxLtvBps, uint16 newLiqLtvBps)` | owner | Sets the borrow limit and the line for new loans, inside hard-coded bounds. |
| `setAuctionParams(uint16 newPenaltyBps, uint16 newAuctionFloorBps, uint32 newAuctionDuration)` | owner | Sets the penalty, floor and duration for loans opened or rolled from now on, inside bounds. |
| `setMinLoan(uint128 newMinLoan)` | owner | Sets the minimum loan, 1 to 100,000 MUSD. |
| `setGuardian(address newGuardian)` | owner | Sets the guardian. |
| `setBorrowsPaused(bool paused)` | guardian or owner | Pauses or resumes new borrows, lock creation and the cash-out part of a roll. Nothing else can be paused. |
| `rescueToken(address token, address to, uint256 amount)` | owner | Moves stray ERC-20 tokens. For MUSD and BTC only the balance above what claimants are owed. Never veBTC. |
| `rescuePosition(uint256 tokenId, address to)` | owner | Moves a veBTC position that arrived without becoming a position. Refuses any recorded position. |
| `transferOwnership(address newOwner)`, `acceptOwnership()` | owner, then the new owner | Two-step ownership transfer (OpenZeppelin Ownable2Step). |

## CreditMarkets

Offers, one market per Thursday, and the ERC-1155 credit units whose token id is the Thursday. Every state-changing function runs under one reentrancy lock.

### Lender and holder functions

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `postOffer(uint64 maturity, uint16 aprBps, uint128 amount)` | anyone | Escrows exactly `amount` MUSD as a fixed-rate offer for a valid Thursday within the longest term. Pausable. |
| `cancelOffer(uint256 offerId)` | the offer's maker | Refunds the unfilled part. Never paused. |
| `claimUnits(uint64 maturity)` | anyone with units waiting | Mints the caller's recorded units for that date. |
| `withdrawCollected(uint64 maturity, uint256 units)` | any holder, after the date | Moves units into the caller's Claim and pays its share of what is collected so far. Zero units pays what arrived since. |
| `finalize(uint64 maturity)` | anyone | Closes a date that has passed, had loans, and has none open. Freezes the redemption totals. |
| `redeem(uint64 maturity, uint256 units)` | any holder, after `finalize` | Burns the caller's units and pays their share of MUSD (at most 1 per unit) and BTC. |
| `safeTransferFrom`, `safeBatchTransferFrom`, `setApprovalForAll` | holders | Standard ERC-1155 transfers and approvals (OpenZeppelin). |

### LoanManager-only functions

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fill(uint64 maturity, uint256[] offerIds, uint128[] amounts, uint16 maxAprBps, address recipient)` | LoanManager | Fills up to 8 listed offers for one loan, records each lender's and the treasury's units, and sends the principal. |
| `credit(uint64 maturity, uint256 musdAmount, bool closesLoan)` | LoanManager | Books MUSD into a date, measured as it arrives. |
| `creditBtc(uint64 maturity, uint256 btcAmount, bool closesLoan)` | LoanManager | Books BTC from a settled loan into a date. |

| Function | Who may call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `setMinOffer(uint128 newMinOffer)` | owner | Minimum offer, 1 to 1,000,000 MUSD. |
| `setMaxTermSeconds(uint32 newMaxTermSeconds)` | owner | Longest term, 1 to 4 weeks. |
| `setTreasury(address newTreasury)` | owner | Receiver of future fee units. |
| `setGuardian(address newGuardian)` | owner | Sets the guardian. |
| `setOffersPaused(bool paused)` | guardian or owner | Pauses or resumes `postOffer` only. |
| `setURI(string newUri)` | owner | Metadata URI template for every date's units. |
| `rescueToken(address token, address to, uint256 amount)` | owner | Moves a stray ERC-20 that is neither MUSD nor BTC. |
| `transferOwnership(address newOwner)`, `acceptOwnership()` | owner, then the new owner | Two-step ownership transfer. |
| `renounceOwnership()` | nobody | Always reverts `RenounceDisabled`. |

## PassThrough

An external library that holds LoanManager's calls into Mezo's voters: `castVote`, `forwardRewards` and `clearForSettle`. It exists only to keep LoanManager under the contract size limit. LoanManager runs it with `delegatecall`, after its own borrower and custody checks and under its reentrancy lock. It keeps no state, and its events and errors are LoanManager's own, so logs carry LoanManager's address.
