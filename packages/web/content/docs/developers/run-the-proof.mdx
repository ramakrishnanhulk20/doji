Everything Doji claims can be checked with commands in the repository. Each one says what it does not cover.

## The live proof pages

Each page lists every transaction with an explorer link and the chain reads that check it.

- [Wave 2 live, 4 November 2026](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/wave2-live-2026-11-04.md): the Wave 2 deploy, two offers parked in Mezo's sMUSD vault and a borrow paid out of the vault, the pay-down demo loan 411 with PayDown as its reward operator and incentives posted on both its pools, and a sale of credit units on UnitMarket. Mezo pays an epoch's incentives once it ends, so the first harvests ran on 5 November: the keeper's on #411 and one from the website on #412. Both are on the page.
- [Quests, 6 November 2026](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/quests-2026-11-06.md): the Faucet's deploy, its first claim and every refusal, the claim route's live claim, the Faucet's filling, offers 5 to 8, seed loans #415 to #417 and #412's repayment, with what the page does not prove.
- [Load check, 6 November 2026](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/load-check-2026-11-06.md): `bash packages/contracts/script/load-fork.sh` fills a fork of Mezo testnet and runs the keeper and the app's pages at 300 offers, 300 loans and 100 live auctions. It sends nothing to the live chain and takes about an hour.
- [Prove it on Wave 2, 4 November 2026](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/prove-it-2026-11-04.md): the one-command proof on the Wave 2 pair, 12 of 12, including a borrow paid out of an offer parked in sMUSD (the vault's parked MUSD fell from 60.00 to 50.00) and PayDown named as the reward operator.
- [Live paths, 4 November 2026](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/live-paths-2026-11-04.md), on the 20 October pair: a veBTC locked directly on Mezo, deposited, borrowed against, repaid and taken back; fresh offers for the next three Thursdays; and a lender paid out after two settles. It also records why `claimRewards` was refused there (F36), the reason the Wave 2 claim checks `isReward` instead.

## Before you start

- [Foundry](https://book.getfoundry.sh) (`forge` and `cast`), bash and Python 3.
- The repository with its two submodules (forge-std and OpenZeppelin Contracts):

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji.git
cd doji
git submodule update --init
```

## 1. The prove-it command (live testnet)

One command exercises the main promise against the deployed contracts on Mezo testnet: lock a little test BTC, borrow the 10 MUSD minimum at a fixed rate from the cheapest open offer, check the loan on chain, attack it as a stranger, repay in full and take the position back. By default it acts on the Wave 2 pair in `deployments/31611-wave2.json`, where it adds four checks labelled W1 to W4:

- W1: when the cheapest offer for the loan's Thursday is parked in Mezo's sMUSD vault, CreditMarkets' `parkedPrincipal()` falls by exactly the amount taken from that offer and the borrower's plain MUSD rises by the principal, both read from the node at the block before the borrow and at the borrow block. When the cheapest offer is not parked, W1 prints SKIP with the reason and the verdict counts only the checks that ran.
- W2: a stranger's `setRewardOperator(position, itself)` is refused with NotBorrower, simulated and never sent.
- W3: the borrower's `setRewardOperator(position, PayDown)` is sent and `positionOf(position).rewardOperator` reads PayDown.
- W4: after the position is taken back, `positionOf(position)` reads borrower zero and reward operator zero.

It sends real testnet transactions from a borrower wallet you control, and needs bash, Python 3 and Foundry. Make a fresh testnet wallet with `cast wallet new`. Copy `packages/contracts/.env.example` to `packages/contracts/.env` and fill in only `BORROWER_PRIVATE_KEY` and `BORROWER_ADDRESS` for that wallet; the other wallets in the file belong to the deploy and seed scripts. It needs at least 0.0008 test BTC (0.0005 to lock, the rest for gas) from the [faucet](https://faucet.test.mezo.org), and a few cents of MUSD for the interest. The faucet gives only BTC, so get the MUSD by borrowing 10 MUSD on [Borrow](/borrow) with the same wallet, or by swapping BTC for MUSD in Mezo's testnet app at [testnet.mezo.org](https://testnet.mezo.org). A [starter kit](/docs/getting-started/quests) covers both, if you import this wallet into a wallet app and claim. The script checks both balances before it sends anything.

```bash
bash packages/contracts/script/prove-it.sh
DEPLOYMENT_FILE=deployments/31611.json bash packages/contracts/script/prove-it.sh   # the 20 October pair, eight checks
```

It prints one PASS, FAIL or SKIP line per check and an explorer link for every transaction it sends. The [4 November 2026 run](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/prove-it-2026-11-04.md) on the Wave 2 pair printed, among the rest:

```text
  == Checks on live position 413, block 16566019
  PASS MUSD arrived: 10.00 MUSD moved from CreditMarkets to the borrower in the borrow transaction (loan principal 10.00)
  PASS LoanManager holds position 413 for the borrower, loan Active, 10.0124 MUSD due 2026-11-12
  PASS collateralValue matches the lock times the oracle: 0.0005 BTC x 78384.23 = 39.19 MUSD
  == Attacks, simulated on the live state as a stranger, never sent
  PASS a stranger's withdrawCollateral is refused: NotBorrower
  PASS startAuction on a healthy, on-time loan is refused: NotLiquidatable
  PASS settle on a healthy, on-time loan is refused: NoAuction
  == Wave 2: the sMUSD vault and the reward operator
  PASS W1 the loan came out of offer 1, parked in Mezo's sMUSD vault: parkedPrincipal fell from 60.00 to 50.00 MUSD, by the 10.00 taken from the offer, and the borrower's MUSD rose by 10.00 (loan principal 10.00)
  PASS W2 a stranger's setRewardOperator naming itself is refused, simulated and never sent: NotBorrower
  PASS W3 the borrower named PayDown 0xf0dbbD5167F9852234883aD58109dC80692a18DE reward operator of position 413 and positionOf reads it back
  == Repaying 10.0124 MUSD in full and taking the position back
  == After the repayment, block 16566030

```

The earlier runs on the 20 October pair, on [20 October](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/prove-it-2026-10-20.md) and [23 October](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/prove-it-2026-10-23.md), each ended with `Doji promise proven: 8 of 8 checks passed`.

If a run stops after the borrow, it prints the position and block, and `bash packages/contracts/script/prove-it.sh --resume POSITION BLOCK` finishes the checks and the repayment on that position. The 20 October record shows exactly this happening: one of the nodes behind Mezo's public RPC answered null for a receipt, so the script now asks again up to 15 times before it counts a transaction as failed.

Each run costs gas, a few cents of MUSD interest, and 0.0005 test BTC that stays locked in the returned veBTC position until the loan date. It does not prove auctions, settle or redemption, a PayDown harvest, or behaviour while the oracle is stale. The fork tests and the example loans cover those.

## 2. The fork tests (real Mezo state)

From `packages/contracts`:

```bash
forge test --match-path "test/fork/*" -vv
```

They fork Mezo mainnet and testnet through the public RPCs named in `foundry.toml`, so they need no key. Public nodes fail at times: a dropped request shows as an HTTP error such as 504 or a reset connection, or as `EvmError: Revert` when it was inside a call to a Mezo contract. To switch node for one run, set `MEZO_MAINNET_RPC` (or `MEZO_TESTNET_RPC` for the testnet suites):

```bash
MEZO_MAINNET_RPC=https://mezo.drpc.org forge test --match-path "test/fork/*"
```

Other public mainnet nodes are `https://mezo-mainnet.boar.network` (the default) and `https://mainnet.mezo.public.validationcloud.io`. A value in the shell wins over `packages/contracts/.env`, only the node's host is logged, and each suite checks the chain id after the switch, so a mainnet variable pointed at testnet fails at once. The run of 6 November 2026 on dRPC ended with:

```text
Ran 7 test suites in 179.41s (359.02s CPU time): 21 tests passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped (21 total tests)
```

| Suite | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| [ForkSmoke.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/ForkSmoke.t.sol) | The tests can read a live veBTC position and the MUSD BTC price on mainnet and testnet, with the right `locked()` layout on each. |
| [CoreSliceFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/CoreSliceFork.t.sol) | A real mainnet position's owner deposits, borrows, repays, withdraws and borrows again from a lender's offer, and after the date the lender redeems the full face. |
| [ResolutionFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/ResolutionFork.t.sol) | An unsafe loan sells half way down its curve; an unsold auction settles the real lock and lenders redeem Bitcoin; a real vote is cleared through the real PoolsVoter once the epoch turns; a loan rolls. |
| [PassThroughFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/PassThroughFork.t.sol) | The borrower votes, collects a real incentive, extends the lock and claims from the real fee distributor through LoanManager; a BoostVoter vote leaves veBTC's voted flag alone; a permanent position voted on both voters (mainnet, where ValidatorsVoter exists) is cleared and settled; `lockAndBorrow` mints a real testnet position into LoanManager; real pool fees reach the borrower. |
| [SavingsFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/SavingsFork.t.sol) | On mainnet, a lender parks two offers in the real sMUSD vault; a real position's owner borrows from the first and is paid exactly the principal out of the vault, and the lender cancels the second and gets exactly its amount back. |
| [FaucetFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/FaucetFork.t.sol) | On testnet, with the real MUSD token: a new Faucet pays a fresh address real MUSD and native BTC, a recipient the real MUSD refuses leaves the claim whole, and the views match the release rule on the fork's clock. |
| [PayDownFork.t.sol](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/packages/contracts/test/fork/PayDownFork.t.sol) | On testnet, against Mezo's real Router and pools: a harvest sells a BTC reward through the real BTC/MUSD pool at or above the oracle floor, and reverts once a large swap pushes that pool 5% under the oracle; a borrower's `harvestAndSwap` sells an mT reward at the borrower's minimum, and reverts once that pool is pushed 5%. |

Each suite ends with a line that starts "Suite result: ok." when every test in it passes, and prints FAIL with the reason when one does not.

What they do not cover: the live Skip oracle and Mezo's BTC precompile cannot run inside a local fork, so the price is read from the node once at the fork block and then mocked or moved by hand, and a stand-in replaces the BTC token wherever BTC moves. PayDownFork uses stand-ins for veBTC, PoolsVoter and the reward contract so a week of rewards can be owed at once. Yield arriving in the vault between a post and a fill, a vault owner changing its strategy, real buyers and keepers, positions owned by contracts, and grant or managed positions are not covered either.

## 3. Unit, fuzz, invariant and audit tests

From `packages/contracts`:

```bash
forge test --no-match-path "test/fork/*"
forge test --match-path "test/audit/*" -vv
```

The first runs everything that needs no network. On 6 November 2026 it ended with:

```text
Ran 26 test suites in 46.99s (196.68s CPU time): 463 tests passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped (463 total tests)
```

Fuzz tests run 1,000 cases each and invariant tests 256 runs of depth 64 (`foundry.toml`). The audit suite holds the tests written for the [audit findings](/docs/security/audit) of both reviews. Every test file opens with a comment that says what it does not cover.

## 4. The explorer verification of both pairs

Every Doji contract is verified on Mezo's testnet explorer, and one script checks it from the deployment record. From `packages/contracts`:

```bash
bash script/verify-testnet.sh deployments/31611-wave2.json
bash script/verify-testnet.sh                 # the 20 October pair, deployments/31611.json
```

It encodes each contract's constructor arguments from the record, compares them byte for byte with the contract's real creation transaction, and submits CreditMarkets, LoanManager, PayDown and UnitMarket (when the record lists them) and then PassThrough. A contract the explorer already shows as verified is skipped, so it is safe to run again, and it exits non-zero if any contract ends unverified. It needs no key.

## 5. The website test

The site itself was driven in a headless Chrome against Mezo testnet with a test wallet, clicking through Borrow, My loans and Lend. Every transaction was sent from the page and checked on chain through both the RPC and the explorer. The record, with every transaction link, is [docs/proof/ui-live-2026-10-20.md](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/ui-live-2026-10-20.md). To repeat it, follow the [Quick start](/docs/getting-started) with your own wallet and open each transaction on the explorer.

The twelve attacks of [Try to break it](/docs/security/try-to-break-it) also run from the command line, read-only, with no key. From `packages/web` after `npm ci`:

```bash
npx tsx scripts/check-attacks.ts
```

It prints one line per attack with the contract's refusal and the block it ran on, then a total of how many were refused with the expected error.

## 6. The Faucet and the quests

The Faucet has its own live proof. From `packages/contracts`:

```bash
bash script/faucet-live.sh
```

It reads the deployment back from the chain, checks that the relayer is no other wallet of Doji's, funds the Faucet with one kit, has the relayer claim for a fresh address, and shows each refusal: a second claim, a stranger, a Faucet too short for a kit, and a paused Faucet, which it then resumes. It records every transaction in a progress file, so a rerun sends nothing: it repeats the chain read-back and reprints what it recorded. On 6 November 2026 a rerun left the deployer's nonce at 119 and the relayer's at 2. Its record is the [quests proof page](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/quests-2026-11-06.md).

That script needs Doji's own deployer and relayer keys and the private progress file, so a stranger cannot repeat it. These need no key and send nothing. From `packages/contracts`:

```bash
cast call 0xCd80D2ef1D47db5c5bc784DE5Ac7a980F8E97BAa "claimsOpen()(uint256)" --rpc-url https://rpc.test.mezo.org
cast call 0xCd80D2ef1D47db5c5bc784DE5Ac7a980F8E97BAa "claimed(address)(bool)" 0xCFD41eb3Cd90BB5E8e583182333a8Dc2DB0992B4 --rpc-url https://rpc.test.mezo.org
forge test --match-path "test/unit/Faucet*.t.sol"
forge test --match-path "test/fork/FaucetFork.t.sol"
```

The first `forge test` line ran 72 tests on 6 November 2026: every revert, hostile recipients, a smart-contract wallet, and random claim times across days. From `packages/web` after `npm ci`:

```bash
npx tsx scripts/check-faucet-route.ts
npx tsx scripts/check-quests.ts
```

The first runs the claim route against mocks, with no network and no real key, and ends with `all checks passed`. The second runs the quest engine on fixtures and then read-only on the live chain, and ends with `all quest checks passed`.

What these do not cover: a claim through the real Cloudflare widget, the route on the production site, or a same-nonce replacement on Mezo's own node. The [proof page](https://github.com/ramakrishnanhulk20/doji/blob/main/docs/proof/quests-2026-11-06.md) lists the rest.
