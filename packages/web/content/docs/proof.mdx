Doji asks you to take nothing on trust. Each answer below points to a screen on this site, a file in the open source repository, and a transaction on Mezo's testnet explorer where one exists.

## Can I try it with only a wallet?

Yes. A new wallet with no gas claims a free starter kit on [/quests](/quests), and Doji pays the fee. Every seal in the ledger comes from a contract log or one public bonus file, with no database.

## Does it really run on Mezo?

veBTC is the collateral and MUSD is the loan. Doji calls veBTC, PoolsVoter, the veBTC fee distributor, MUSD, MUSD's PriceFeed and the BTC token, ValidatorsVoter on mainnet (it does not exist on testnet, so there Doji passes votes to PoolsVoter only), and on the Wave 2 pair Mezo's sMUSD vault and pool Router. It does nothing without them.

## Is it a real market, and who pays?

A two-sided market that extends what MUSD can do: veBTC holders borrow without ending their lock, and MUSD holders lend at a fixed rate to a fixed date. The protocol earns 10% of the interest and a 5% penalty on failed loans.

## Is my Bitcoin safe in it?

Immutable contracts, a written rulebook with 82 rules, fuzz and invariant tests, fork tests against real Mezo state, two independent reviews with every finding settled, and one command that proves the main promise live. The Faucet, which holds test tokens only, had the reviewer's threat pass and the test suite, not a separate audit.
