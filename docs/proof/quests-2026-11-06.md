## What this page does not prove

- A preview build's route on Vercel. No preview deployment exists. [check-faucet-route.ts](../../packages/web/scripts/check-faucet-route.ts) proves the refusal for `VERCEL_ENV` preview and development on mocks.
- A same-nonce replacement of a stuck claim on Mezo's own node, which is proven on mocks only.
- The even release through a full day, a hostile recipient and a smart-contract wallet. Those are in [Faucet.t.sol](../../packages/contracts/test/unit/Faucet.t.sol) and [FaucetInvariants.t.sol](../../packages/contracts/test/unit/FaucetInvariants.t.sol), not on the live chain.
- A purchase or a settlement in the four auctions above. On 14 November all four were open and nobody had bought one.
- A fix for MetaMask's built-in phone browser. Whether Doji's way round issue 47773 also works there is not tested.
- That one wallet is one person. Turnstile, one claim per address and the daily limit slow a script down and do not prove one human.
- How the site holds up with many testers at once. That is the [load check](load-check-2026-11-06.md), run on a fork.
- Anything about the worth of EXP. EXP has no cash value. Doji may use it in future under terms published then. Nothing is promised.
