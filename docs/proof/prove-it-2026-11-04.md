# Prove-it run on Mezo testnet, 2026-11-04

The same command, `bash packages/contracts/script/prove-it.sh`, run for the first time against the Wave 2 contracts on Mezo testnet, which it now uses by default: LoanManager 0xdB407A06E5CedF2fd4f8C5C557338908B2776978, CreditMarkets 0x89Dc7C4a7Cbfddf3Cb7a00b5f3fEFaB47aC61a1f, PayDown 0xf0dbbD5167F9852234883aD58109dC80692a18DE, PassThrough 0x895187e9501E424774d93A0931D36b4be99E58A9, deployed 4 November 2026 at block 16,564,749 (record: [deployments/31611-wave2.json](../../packages/contracts/deployments/31611-wave2.json)). It locks a little test Bitcoin, borrows MUSD at a fixed rate from the cheapest open offer, checks the result on chain, tries three attacks as a stranger, repays and takes the position back. Every transaction links to Mezo's explorer. The output below is unedited. It ran in one pass, with no resume.

On the Wave 2 pair it adds four checks, labelled W1 to W4:

- W1: the cheapest offer for 12 November was offer 1, parked in Mezo's sMUSD vault. Read from the node at the block before the borrow and at the borrow block, CreditMarkets' `parkedPrincipal()` fell by exactly the 10 MUSD taken from that offer, and the borrower's plain MUSD rose by the 10 MUSD principal. When the cheapest offer is not parked, W1 prints SKIP with the reason and is left out of the count.
- W2: a stranger's `setRewardOperator(413, itself)` is refused with NotBorrower, simulated on the live state and never sent.
- W3: the borrower's `setRewardOperator(413, PayDown)` is sent, and `positionOf(413).rewardOperator` reads PayDown.
- W4: once the position is taken back, `positionOf(413)` reads borrower zero and reward operator zero, so the operator left with the position.

Before this run the same command passed 12 of 12 on a local anvil fork of testnet, 8 of 8 there on the 20 October pair with `DEPLOYMENT_FILE=deployments/31611.json`, and 11 of 11 with `skipped: W1` once a cheaper plain offer was posted on the fork.
